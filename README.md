# quake2RE
This is a backrooms style mod for quake 2 rerelease.

Installation:
Unzip folder into Quake 2 Rerelease Directory
Open Folder
Run Quake 2 Shortcut


This includes:
- A sanity bar that you must manage
- 5 world events
- 5 monsters
- 5 tools
- 5 abilities

5 world events:
Dark spots that drain your sanity while you are in them
A Fake Exit sign that will drain your sanity and dissapear if you get too close
A endlessly looping hallway that will drain your sanity on each loop
A watercooler that will restore your sanity
A safe area that will slowly restore sanity

5 monsters:
The Watcher - Drains sanity if you look at him, spawns at <=80% sanity.
The Wireframe - Hears your loud movements, tracks you perfectly when close. <=40% sanity
The Snitch - Hears your normal movements, summons the wireframe nearby your location. <=40% sanity
The Skinwalker - A humanoid figure that stands still, get too close and you will be hurt and lose sanity.
The Mannequin - A weeping angel style creature. If it touches you drains sanity, if sanity is 0 it will kill you.

5 tools:
Almond Water - on the go sanity restoration
Flashlight - used to prevent sanity drain in darkspots
Alarm Clock - used to lure the wireframe
Detector - Helps you find things like supplies
EMF Reader - notifies you when monsters like the snitch or the mannequin are near

5 abilities:
Sprint
Hide
Sneak
Lean
Close Eyes
